Frequently asked questions

Is Quick PDF Converter really free?

Yes. All four tools are free, with no sign-up and no watermark on your documents.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion runs inside your browser on your own device. Your documents never leave it.

Why does my PDF say it is a scanned image?

Some PDFs are photos or scans of paper pages: they contain pictures, not text, so there is no text to convert. Such files need text recognition (OCR) first.

Can I convert a password-protected PDF?

Yes. You will be asked for the password; it is only used to open the file and is never saved.

Which file types are supported?

PDF files for PDF to Word and PDF to Excel, .docx files for Word to PDF, and .xlsx or .csv files for Excel to PDF.
